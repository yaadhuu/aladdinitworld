# Adding and updating MacBooks: one-page guide

**Open the admin:** go to the admin address the CEO gave you (it ends in `.sanity.studio`; bookmark it) and sign in with your own login. Never share your login. Changes show on the website within about 2 minutes.

You will see these on the left: **Stock**, **Home page: featured MacBooks**, **Customer reviews** and **Questions and answers**.

## Add a new MacBook
1. Click **Stock**, then **All MacBooks**, then the **+** (new) button at the top.
2. Fill in the five tabs at the top of the form: **Basics**, **Condition**, **Price & status**, **Photos**, **Description**. Use the drop-down lists wherever you see one. Each box has a short hint under it.
3. Add **1 to 6 photos** of this exact MacBook. The first photo is the main one. In the Photos tab, click the **Upload** arrow and choose **Take photo** to use the camera and add the picture at once (on a phone it uses the back camera; the first time, allow camera access). If a list does not have what you need (chip, memory, storage, screen), pick **Other (type it)** and type it in the box that appears.
4. Click **Publish** (bottom right). If something is missing, a red message tells you what to fix. The MacBook goes live only after it is published.

## Add a similar MacBook quickly
Open a MacBook that is already in the list, click the **three dots** at the bottom right, choose **Duplicate**, change what is different (photos, price, battery), then **Publish**.

## Change a price or any detail
Open the MacBook, change it, click **Publish**.

## Mark a MacBook as Sold
Open it, go to **Price & status**, set **Status** to **Sold**, click **Publish**. It disappears from the website. Do not delete it.

## Reserve a MacBook
Set **Status** to **Reserved** and publish. It stays on the website with a "Reserved" tag.

## Choose the MacBooks shown on the home page
Open **Home page: featured MacBooks**. Click **Add item**, search and pick a MacBook that is already in Stock, then **Publish**. You cannot create a new MacBook here. Drag the items to change the order. The home page shows up to 6. To remove one, use the three dots on that item, then **Publish**. A MacBook marked Sold is skipped by itself.

## If something looks wrong
- **A MacBook is not on the website:** check its Status is Available or Reserved, it has at least 1 photo, and you clicked Publish. Then wait 2 minutes.
- **I changed something by mistake:** open the MacBook, click the **three dots** at the bottom right, choose **Review changes** or **History**, and go back to the earlier version.
- **A MacBook was deleted:** tell the CEO straight away.
- **The admin will not open or sign in:** tell the CEO. Do not try to fix it yourself.

## Rules
- Photos must be of this exact MacBook, not stock pictures.
- Be honest about the outside condition and battery. Customers see it.
- The warranty comes from the grade automatically. Do not write it anywhere.
- Never put customer names, phone numbers, serial numbers or what we paid anywhere in the admin. The website is public.
- When a person leaves the team, the CEO removes their login the same day.

## Questions and answers on the website
Open **Questions and answers** on the left. Click **+** to add one: type the question and a short answer, then **Publish**. **Position** decides the order (1 shows first). The home page shows the first 6, and a button shows the rest. To change an answer, open it, edit, **Publish**. To remove one, open it and use Delete. Only write what is true today (warranty, prices, hours).

## Automatic clean-up
Sold MacBooks are deleted by themselves after 15 days, together with their photos, to keep the storage free. You can change the number of days, or switch it off, under **Automatic clean-up** (Administrators only). The 15 days are counted from the last time that MacBook was changed. A deleted MacBook cannot be brought back, so if you may need one again, change its status to Available instead.

## Customer reviews
Open **Customer reviews**, click **+**, type the customer name, where they wrote it, the stars and their own words, then **Publish**. Only add real reviews from real customers, and keep their words as they wrote them. **Position** 1 shows first. The home page scrolls through them and the Reviews page lists them all.

## Preview before you publish
Open a MacBook, a question or a review, click the **three dots** at the bottom right and choose **Preview on website**. The website opens in a new tab showing your changes **before** they are published, with a black bar at the bottom saying "Preview". Check it, close the tab (or click **Exit preview**), then come back and click **Publish**. If it says the link is not valid, open it again from the Studio.
